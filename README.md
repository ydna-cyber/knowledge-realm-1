# 🧙‍♂️ Knowledge Realm — An RPG Study Assistant

**Knowledge Realm** is a Gamified Active-Recall Study Tool built to help students learn effectively while battling the two biggest enemies of studying: **procrastination** and **burnout**.

---

## 🎯 The Philosophy Behind the Game

Studying is often seen as a chore. Students generally face two major hurdles:

1. **Procrastination & Low Engagement**: Rereading textbooks or doomscrolling when frustrated leads to zero retention. *Knowledge Realm* turns studying into an engaging RPG battle system (inspired by games like Prodigy and Blooket), where answering questions correctly unlocks powerful spell attacks, cosmetic rewards, and character progression.
2. **Burnout**: Pushing too hard without breaks causes mental fatigue, loss of motivation, and physical stress. Studies show that human focus peaks around 60–70 minutes. To prevent burnout, *Knowledge Realm* features a mandatory **60-Minute Study Timer** followed by a **10-Minute Lockout Break** to ensure you rest your eyes and recharge before continuing.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- ⚔️ **Spell-Based Combat Loop**: Select your action (Basic Attack, Heal, Poison DoT, or Freeze), then answer a question to cast it!
- 🤖 **Gemini AI Integration**: Type in any topic (e.g., "Organic Chemistry", "World War II") to generate dynamic study questions on the fly using your Gemini API key.
- 📚 **Built-in Question Database**: Works fully offline with over 45 pre-loaded questions across Math, Science, and History.
- 🧘 **Built-in Anti-Burnout Timer**: Tracks your 60-minute study session and enforces a non-skippable 10-minute relaxation pause.
- 🎩 **Wardrobe & Shop Customization**: Equip 18+ unique hats, weapons, and pets that boost your hero's health, damage, and lifesteal stats.
- 📖 **Learning Log**: Review all past questions, answers, and explanations to reinforce your memory.

---

## 🚀 How to Play

1. Open the game in your browser.
2. Enter a study topic at the top bar (e.g., *Algebra*, *Space*, *European History*).
3. *(Optional)* Add your **Gemini API Key** in the **Settings** tab to generate custom questions for any topic.
4. Choose your spell, answer the study question, and defeat the monsters to earn gold and MP!
